Privacy and measurement

This site uses Adobe Analytics to understand visits and interactions, and Adobe Target to test portfolio page variations. These measurement tools load only after you choose “Allow measurement”. Declining does not prevent access to the portfolio.

Measurement includes fixed page and section names, navigation and contact-link clicks, approximate scroll depth, device and browser information, and Adobe-generated visitor identifiers. A click on Email or LinkedIn indicates an intent to leave this site; it does not establish that a message was sent or received.

The implementation does not send form contents, email addresses, passwords, cookie values, arbitrary PDF URLs, or full URL query strings to Analytics. Approved campaign codes can be measured separately. Adobe processes technical information needed to deliver and measure its services.

SignalScope Lab is a demonstration area. Its simulated interactions use a separate Analytics report suite and are excluded from portfolio results.

Your preference is stored in this browser for 180 days. Use “Change measurement choice” on this page to change it. Declining after allowing measurement reloads the page to stop the loaded measurement tools. This stops future measurement; it does not remove data already processed. PDF viewing uses Adobe’s PDF service separately when you choose to open a document.
